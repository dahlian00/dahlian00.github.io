About

Risa Shinoda 篠田理沙

I am an Assistant Professor at the Yoichi Sato Lab, University of Tokyo. I received my Ph.D. in Agricultural Engineering from Kyoto University in 2025, M.S. in Agricultural Engineering in 2022, and B.S. in Agricultural Engineering in 2020, all from Kyoto University.

My research applies computer vision to agriculture and animal ecology — building datasets and benchmarks for species and individual identification, vision–language models for agricultural understanding, and multimodal (visual, textual, and acoustic) learning from biological data.

Research interests: animal identification & recognition · agricultural computer vision · vision–language models · multimodal (visual–textual–acoustic) learning · dataset & benchmark construction

CV

Risa Shinoda 篠田理沙

I am an Assistant Professor at the Yoichi Sato Lab, University of Tokyo. My research applies computer vision to agriculture and animal ecology — building datasets and benchmarks for species and individual identification, vision–language models for agricultural understanding, and multimodal (visual, textual, and acoustic) learning from biological data.

I completed my Ph.D. in Agricultural Engineering at Kyoto University in 2025, and my M.S. (2022) and B.S. (2020) in the same department.

Research interests:

  • Animal identification & recognition
  • Agricultural computer vision
  • Vision–language models
  • Multimodal (visual–textual–acoustic) learning
  • Dataset & benchmark construction
